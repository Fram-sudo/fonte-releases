Your workout tracker, no subscription.

Free, no account, no ads, and everything works offline. Workouts, routines, records, stats, and a Solo Leveling style level and rank system.

Android Android 8 or newer
Download Fonte
  1. Open the downloaded Fonte.apk file.
  2. Allow the install if Android asks.
  3. If Play Protect shows up: More details, then Install anyway. Fonte is not on the Play Store, this is expected.

Updates then arrive right inside the app. Home Screen widgets with settings: week, rank, goal, boss and current workout.

iPhone iOS 16.4 or newer, with Safari
Open the web app
  1. Open the web app in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (the square with an arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then open Fonte from its icon.

Your data stays on your phone. A backup moves from Android to iPhone and back. No widget on iPhone: only App Store apps can have one.

What's new

Update of November 4, 2026 · Android and iPhone

1.0.27 · photo, skull and measurements

Your photo on your card

In the hunter profile or in Friends, tap the circle to choose a photo and crop it. It goes with your card: your friends see it in their list, on your profile and in challenge rankings. Cards from earlier versions still open.

A skull for your bosses

When you have a boss on an exercise, a skull shows next to its name: in workouts, routines, the exercise list and history. The boss card tells you which set beats it, with your weights.

Your measurements, not ours

Create the measurements you want to track: biceps, chest, waist, thigh, calf, or any name. Each one has its chart and history. Your old entries (shoulders, arm, waist) are kept.

Bodyweight and duration

Each input type has an example ("Duration: plank, side plank, wall sit"), and you can change the type of any exercise from its page. For planks, a timer counts the set and checks it off at the end.

A clearer rest timer

On Android, the notification shows the set first, then the exercise name on two lines: nothing gets cut off anymore. On iPhone, a note explains why to keep Fonte on screen during rest: iOS mutes the end sound when you switch apps. Once read, it doesn't come back.

And on January 1…

A little surprise is waiting when you open Fonte. This version is enough.

1.0.26 · fix for 1.0.25

Clearer winter bosses

The weight shown on a winter boss was an estimated 1RM (what you could lift just once), not the load to use. Many read it as a load. The boss card now shows the sets that beat it, with your own weights: if you train with 14 kg against a "Shoulder press 25 kg" boss, it suggests 14 kg × 24 or 16 kg × 17. Every set that gets close brings its health bar down.

1.0.25 · winter season

Winter Arc, until February 28

The winter season has its own card on the Home screen. There are 8 steps to earn, one for each week you reach your workout goal. The season lasts 17 weeks, so you can miss a few.

Winter challenges

Every week, 2 new winter challenges: a weekend workout, 2 records, 10,000 kg lifted, a one-hour workout... Each one gives quest XP.

Three winter bosses

They come one after the other: the Yeti right now, the Blizzard on December 1 at the earliest, the Winter King in January at the earliest. Each one targets an estimated 1RM 2.5 to 4% above yours, on an exercise you do often: its card tells you which sets beat it. It gives boss XP.

Forged by winter

All 8 steps and the Winter King defeated before the end of February: you earn the "Forged by winter" title and its frosted emblem, to show on your hunter profile. They stay yours after the season.

Winter challenge with friends

A ready-made challenge in the Challenges screen: most workouts this month. Like the other challenges, it goes through your friend cards, with no server and no account.

Seasonal effects

A setting in Settings > Appearance, on by default. It only shows from December 1 to January 6, while the snow and the tree are there. Turn it off whenever you like; the Winter Arc and its bosses stay.

And on December 1…

Something is coming to Fonte, with nothing more to install: this version is enough. That's all we'll say.

What Fonte does

Workouts and routines

Folders, supersets, quick set entry, rest timer with sound in your earphones.

931 exercises

A French and English library with images, muscles and technique, plus your own exercises.

Progress

Records, estimated 1RM, suggested next weight, charts, body weight and measurements, a report every month.

Levels and ranks

Every set earns XP: weekly quests, bosses, achievements, from E- up to Monarch.

Friends

Share your card, compare, start challenges, copy a friend's program. No server, just a file.

Your data stays yours

No account, no ads, no trackers. Import from Hevy, backups and Obsidian export.
